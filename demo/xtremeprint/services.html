Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us.

How long does printing take?

Most standard orders (business cards, flyers) take 2-3 business days. Large format projects may take 3-5 days depending on the scope. We always strive to meet your deadlines!

Do you offer delivery in the Vaal?

Yes! We offer free delivery within Sasolburg for all orders over R500. For surrounding areas like Vanderbijlpark and Vereeniging, a small delivery fee may apply.

What file formats do you accept?

We prefer high-resolution PDF, AI (Adobe Illustrator), or CDR (CorelDraw) files with fonts converted to curves. We also accept high-res JPG and PNG files.

Can you help me with design?

Absolutely! Our in-house design team can help you from scratch or tweak your existing files to ensure they are print-ready and look professional.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes, we offer competitive pricing for large volume orders. Please contact us with your specific requirements for a custom bulk-discounted quote.

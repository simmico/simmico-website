Our Story

Passion for print, dedicated to the Vaal community.

Xtreme Print By Design was born out of a simple vision: to provide the Sasolburg and greater Vaal region with world-class printing services that don't compromise on personal touch. Founded by Dawie Steyn, we started as a small design studio and have since grown into a full-service printing powerhouse, trusted by hundreds of local businesses.

We believe that great printing starts with great design. That's why we don't just "hit print" — we work closely with our clients to ensure their branding is consistent, professional, and impactful. Whether you're a startup in Vanderbijlpark needing your first set of business cards or an established firm in Vereeniging looking for a complete signage overhaul, we are here to help.

As members of the Vaal community ourselves, we take immense pride in seeing our work on the streets of Sasolburg and beyond. Our commitment to the region goes beyond business; it's about building lasting relationships and helping our neighbors succeed through quality visual communication. Baie dankie for choosing us as your local printing partner.

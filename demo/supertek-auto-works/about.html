Our Story

Fifteen years of honest automotive work in Alberton — and we're only getting started.

Who We Are

Built on Trust, Driven by Precision

Supertek Auto Works began in 2009 when founder Paulo Ferreira set up a small workshop on Voortrekker Road with one goal: to offer the people of Alberton the kind of honest, expert automotive service that had always been hard to find locally. From day one, our commitment to transparency set us apart — no hidden costs, no unnecessary work, just straight talk and solid results that clients in our community could count on.

Over the years we've grown into one of Alberton's most trusted automotive service centres. Our team has expanded, our equipment has evolved, and our range of services now covers everything from minor services and brake repairs to advanced engine diagnostics. But the core values haven't changed — every vehicle that comes through our doors receives the same level of care and attention to detail, whether it's a daily commuter or a weekend bakkie.

We're proud to be a local business in the truest sense. Most of our clients come from right here in Alberton, Brackenhurst, and New Redruth — and many have been with us for over a decade. We believe that when a business looks after its community, the community looks after the business. That's the Supertek way, and it's why we're still here, still growing, and still putting precision first.

The People Behind the Work

Meet the Supertek Team

Every vehicle that comes through our doors is handled by people who take genuine pride in their craft.

Workshop Manager & Founder

Paulo Ferreira

Paulo has over 18 years of hands-on automotive experience and holds multiple qualifications in vehicle diagnostics and servicing. He oversees every job that comes through the workshop and sets the standard for precision and honesty that defines Supertek.

Senior Technician

Sipho Dlamini

With 12 years under the bonnet, Sipho specialises in engine diagnostics and complex electrical fault-finding. His methodical approach means nothing gets missed, and his clients keep coming back because of the trust he's earned one job at a time.

Service Advisor

Linda Erasmus

Linda is the voice of Supertek — she listens, explains, and ensures every client understands exactly what's happening with their vehicle. With six years at the front desk, she's the reason so many first-time clients become regulars.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Three principles that guide every decision we make, every quote we give, and every car we touch.

Precision

We treat every vehicle as if it were our own. No shortcuts, no guesswork. We use the right tools, the right parts, and we check our work — because your safety depends on it.

Transparency

You see every part we remove, every reading we take, every rand we charge. We call you before any unplanned work. No hidden costs, no inflated markups, no surprises on the invoice.

Community

Alberton is our home. We invest in the trust of our neighbours, one honest service at a time. Our success is built on yours — and we'll never forget that.

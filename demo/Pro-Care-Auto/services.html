Our Professional Services

We offer a comprehensive range of automotive services to keep your vehicle running smoothly. Our workshop is equipped with the latest diagnostic tools and staffed by expert technicians.

Full Vehicle Servicing

Comprehensive oil changes, filter replacements, and point-to-point inspections to maintain your car's longevity and performance. We use high-quality lubricants and genuine parts where possible.

Brake & Suspension

Expert brake pad replacement, disc skimming, and suspension tuning to ensure a smooth and safe ride on South African roads. Safety is our top priority.

Engine Diagnostics

Using modern diagnostic tools to accurately identify and resolve engine faults, saving you time and preventing further damage. We get to the root of the problem quickly.

Major Repairs

From gearbox overhauls to clutch replacements, we handle complex mechanical work with precision and care. No job is too big for our experienced team.

Battery & Electrical

Battery testing and replacement, alternator repairs, and general electrical troubleshooting to keep your car starting reliably every time.

Cooling Systems

Radiator flushes, water pump replacements, and hose inspections to prevent overheating during those hot Gauteng summers.

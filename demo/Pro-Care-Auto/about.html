About Pro Care Auto

Trusted Local Mechanics

We understand that your vehicle is essential to your daily life in Gauteng, whether you are commuting to work or taking the family out for the weekend.

Our team of skilled technicians brings years of experience to every job, ensuring that every bolt is tightened and every system is checked. We pride ourselves on transparent communication and honest pricing for all our neighbours in the Vaal.

We believe in building long-term relationships with our clients. That is why we focus on preventative maintenance, helping you avoid costly repairs down the road and keeping your vehicle in peak condition for longer.

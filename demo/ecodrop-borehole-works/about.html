Our Story

Learn more about our commitment to sustainable water solutions in the Vaal region.

Ecodrop Borehole Works started with a simple vision: to provide the Meyerton community with a reliable and sustainable source of water. As residents of the Vaal ourselves, we saw the growing need for independent water solutions that don't just solve immediate problems but also respect our local environment. We began with just one drilling rig and a lot of determination, and today we are proud to be a leading name in the region.

We believe that water is a precious resource, and our work reflects that belief. Every project we undertake is approached with a blend of technical precision and genuine care for the land. Whether we're drilling for a small suburban garden or a large commercial farm, we treat each site as if it were our own. Our deep roots in the Vaal community have allowed us to build lasting relationships based on trust and the consistent delivery of high-quality results.

Today, our team continues to grow, but our core values remain the same. We are committed to using the best equipment and the most sustainable practices to ensure that your water supply is secure for generations to come. Thank you for choosing Ecodrop Borehole Works – we look forward to serving you and helping our beautiful Vaal region thrive.

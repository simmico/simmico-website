How It Works

Four straightforward steps from first call to flowing water — we keep you informed at every stage.

1

Free Consultation

Call, WhatsApp, or email us. We discuss your needs, answer questions, and give an honest assessment — no obligation.

2

Site Survey

Our geologists assess your land to identify the best drilling point, estimated depth, and yield expectation.

3

Professional Drilling

We mobilise our rigs and complete drilling efficiently — typically 1 to 3 days for residential projects.

4

Testing & Handover

Yield testing, pump installation, and a full briefing on operating and maintaining your new water source.

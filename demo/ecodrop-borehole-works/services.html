Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about boreholes and our services.

How long does it take to drill a borehole?

Most residential drillings take between 1 to 3 days, depending on the geology and the required depth. We strive to be as efficient as possible while maintaining safety standards.

Do I need a permit for a borehole in Meyerton?

Generally, residential boreholes for domestic use do not require a permit, but you must register the borehole with your local municipality. We can assist you with the registration process.

What is yield testing and why is it important?

Yield testing measures how much water your borehole can sustainably produce over time. This is critical for selecting the correct pump and ensuring you don't over-extract and damage the source.

What maintenance does a borehole require?

We recommend a professional check-up every 12 to 24 months. This includes checking the pump's electrical components, testing water pressure, and ensuring there's no buildup in the casing.

Can I use borehole water for drinking?

While borehole water is often clean, we always recommend getting a water quality test done. If necessary, we can install filtration systems to make it safe for human consumption.

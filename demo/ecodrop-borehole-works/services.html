Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about boreholes and our services.

How long does it take to drill a borehole?

Most residential drillings take 1 to 3 days, depending on geology and required depth. We work efficiently while maintaining strict safety standards.

Do I need a permit for a borehole in Meyerton?

Generally, residential boreholes for domestic use do not require a permit, but you must register with your local municipality. We assist with the registration process.

What is yield testing and why is it important?

Yield testing measures how much water your borehole can sustainably produce over time — critical for choosing the correct pump and preventing over-extraction.

What maintenance does a borehole require?

We recommend a professional check-up every 12 to 24 months: pump electrical checks, water pressure testing, and casing inspection.

Can I use borehole water for drinking?

Borehole water is often clean, but we always recommend a water quality test first. If needed, we can recommend and install appropriate filtration systems.

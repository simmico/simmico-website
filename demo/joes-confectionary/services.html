Common Questions

Everything you need to know before you order.

How far in advance should I book a custom cake?+
For standard birthday cakes, we require at least 3-5 days notice. For wedding cakes, we recommend booking 2-3 months in advance to secure your date.
Do you deliver in the Vaal area?+
Yes! We deliver to Vanderbijlpark, Vereeniging, Sasolburg, and Meyerton. Delivery fees depend on the distance and the size of the order.
Can you cater for specific dietary requirements?+
We certainly do our best! We offer gluten-free and eggless options for most of our cakes. Please mention your requirements when booking.
Do you offer cake tasting sessions?+
Yes, for wedding cake inquiries over R1,500, we offer a tasting box with our top 4 flavors for a small fee which is deducted from your final deposit.
Where are you located for collections?+
Our bakery is located at 39 Col Gordon Street, Kollege Park, Vanderbijlpark. You'll see our sign right near the main road!
